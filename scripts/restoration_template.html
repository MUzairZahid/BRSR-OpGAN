Data provenance and what this page represents
Released data, released weights, real outputs.

Every row on this page is a row of the released BRSR test split (Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23010395): the clean target, the stored echo, co-channel interference and AWGN components, and the received signal. The restored signals are the outputs of the released BRSR-OpGAN-D generator weights after the first and the second pass, computed by scripts/export_restoration_samples.py and checked against reference_results/brsr_test_per_sample.csv to within 0.02 dB.

Signals are shown in units of clean RMS with one scale for every trace. Samples are stored in the page as 16-bit integers with a per-trace scale (maximum quantisation error below 3.1 × 10⁻⁵ of each trace's peak), which keeps this single file small enough to load on a phone.

What is measured.

Input and restored SNR follow the evaluation protocol of the BRSR-OpGAN paper (docs/EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md): SNR = 10·log₁₀(mean|x|² / mean|ŷ − x|²) over both channels. The page recomputes them from the embedded samples; the numbers agree with the reference results.

The scene is a metaphor for the additive model of the benchmark. The echo is a delayed copy of the same source waveform (delay τ, in samples); no ranges, Doppler shifts or antenna patterns are simulated. The restorer box stands for the released Self-ONN U-Net generator (Q = 3) applied twice.

Spectrograms use a 64-point Hann window with a 4-sample hop over the complex signal (241 frames, centres 0.315–9.915 µs; bin centres −50 to +48.4 MHz), drawn at native resolution without smoothing. Colours are fixed from −40 to 0 dB relative to the clean spectrogram peak of the selected row; all panels share the scale.

This chapter and the generator chapter use the same design and controls but different signals: the generator page shows newly seeded generator output, this page shows released test rows.

How was this signal made? The generator chapter builds a sample from scratch: waveform, echo, interference and noise, with the SNR under your control.
